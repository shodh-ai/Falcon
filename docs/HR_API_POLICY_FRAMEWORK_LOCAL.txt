SCORING SHEET
The starter is an illustrative institutional-review draft, not an official or
current UGC, PCI, CAS or promotion eligibility policy. Historical API category
inspiration: https://www.ugc.gov.in/e-book/UGC_Regulation/files/basic-html/page791.html
The sample weights, targets, caps and rates below are original illustrative
defaults, not copied regulatory requirements. HR must replace/approve them.

Sample categories: Teaching 40%, Research 30%, Development 15%, Service 15%.
Sample criteria: teaching delivery, teaching resources, research outputs,
projects/supervision, professional development and institutional service.
HR can add/remove/rename categories and criteria for each actual designation
and assessment year; designation is not the employee's login role.

Two scoring modes:
- WEIGHTED_100: criterion points are capped, each category is normalized to its
  category maximum and multiplied by its percentage weight. Weights total 100.
- RAW_POINTS: add capped criterion points without normalization. Category
  weights are ignored. The denominator is the sum of all criterion caps.

Three supported criterion methods (no arbitrary executable formulas):
- COUNT: min(whole-number quantity x points per item, maximum points).
- TARGET: min(progress / target x maximum points, maximum points).
- DIRECT: reviewed score between zero and the criterion maximum.
Intermediate values round to four decimal places; final total to two.
An explicit zero differs from a missing value. Positive claims need an evidence
reference when that rule is enabled. References are not automatically verified.
Invalid numbers, fractional counts, unknown criteria and invalid weights fail
with an explanation. Incomplete previews cannot become saved calculations.

MANUAL CHECK FLOW
1. Sign in locally as scoped HR Admin and select the intended organization.
2. Open Designation API Policies. Loading does not activate any template.
3. Choose an actual designation, assessment year and descriptive policy title.
4. Review every category, criterion, cap, rate/target and evidence requirement.
5. Enter sample values and use Calculate preview. Inspect input, formula,
   raw points, cap, earned points, category contribution and final total.
6. Save draft. This is inactive. Reload/select that version to confirm retention.
7. To publish, provide an institutional approval reference and acknowledge HR's
   review. Unsaved changes must be saved first. Publication is an explicit
   HRAdmin/SuperAdmin action, not automated regulatory or dual approval.
8. With a published policy, select a scoped employee of the matching designation,
   enter complete evidence-backed values, calculate, then save the immutable
   calculation record. Save retries must not create duplicate records.
9. Load recent saved calculations and audit history. Check version, timestamp,
   formulas and checksum. Copy a published version into a new draft to change
   rules; publishing the new version must not alter old saved calculations.
10. Try a read-only HR role and an out-of-scope organization/employee. Write
    actions are denied and employee details remain scope-filtered.

Example using the illustrative weighted template: teaching delivery 80% earns
48/60, resources count 4 earns 20/40; teaching contribution = 68/100 x 40 = 27.2.
Research outputs count 4 earns 20/60 plus projects direct 20/40 => 12 weighted.
Development 15 of 30 hours => 7.5 weighted. Service direct 60/100 => 9 weighted.
Total = 55.7/100, assuming the required evidence references are supplied.
At full caps the same template gives 100/100 weighted or 400/400 raw points.

DATA AND AUTHORIZATION SAFETY
Tenant, entity, current HR role and delegated object scopes are checked on the
server. The browser cannot supply a trusted total, actor or tenant. Creation
and calculation saves use idempotency keys. Draft updates/publication use
If-Match revision checks. Published definitions and calculation snapshots are
immutable; changes create a new policy version. Audit events are append-only.
Existing appraisal list reads no longer recalculate or overwrite saved scores.
Legacy fallback insertions cannot overwrite a concurrently reviewed appraisal.

BOUNDARY: This framework saves CHECKED_CALCULATION_NOT_FINAL_APPRAISAL records.
It does not replace the faculty/HoD/HR final appraisal workflow, grant promotion
eligibility or change payroll. Existing legacy assessments remain separate.
